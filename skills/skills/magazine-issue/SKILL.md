# The office magazine

The realtor assembles the brokerage's magazine and sends it to a printer. library/magazine/ holds past issues (PDFs), the template notes, the printer's specs (printer/), and a README with recurring sections, and the realtor's notes on what worked.

## Every issue starts with
1. Read the last two issues: sections, page count, word counts, tone, how listings and solds are shown.
2. Read the printer's specs and deadline. Work the schedule backwards: print deadline, final proof approval (2 business days before), layout complete, copy final, content brief. Put dates on each.
3. The flat plan (`flatplan-<issue>.md`): a table of every page (or spread) with content, owner (Ivy copy / Mercer layout / realtor to supply), word count, images needed and status. Page count must satisfy the printer's rule (usually a multiple of 4).

## Recurring content (adjust to what past issues actually contain)
- Cover and cover lines; welcome note from the realtor or brokerage.
- Market update with cited stats (source and month under every figure).
- Feature articles: neighbourhood profile, design/home story, local business or event.
- Featured listings and recent solds (from current listings in the website snapshot or provided; sold prices only as permitted).
- Team spotlight; testimonials (real, with permission).
- Back cover: brokerage, contact details, required BCFSA identification.

## Copy handoff to production
One `.md` per article: slug, page(s), headline, deck, byline, body, pull quotes, captions, image list (file names or what's needed) and credit lines. Mark anything not final as [TK: ...].

## Before it goes to print
Run the print-production proofing checklist, then a compliance pass (bcfsa-advertising). The realtor approves the final proof and sends it.
