# BC real estate advertising checklist

Rules change; library/compliance/ holds the current BCFSA guidance the realtor has saved. Cite it (or bcfsa.ca) with a date, and flag anything you couldn't verify.

## Identification (BCFSA Rules on advertising)
- The brokerage's name, as licensed, appears clearly and prominently on every ad: print, signs, social profiles and posts, websites, magazine pages.
- The licensee's name is shown as licensed (no unlicensed nicknames or titles). Team names follow BCFSA team advertising rules and still show the brokerage.
- Personal real estate corporations are named correctly where used.

## Accuracy
- No false, misleading or unverifiable claims: avoid "best", "#1", "guaranteed", "lowest commission", "won't last", unless provable and sourced.
- Measurements: state the source ("per floor plan by ...", "per strata plan", "BC Assessment") and use "approx." where appropriate. Never round in the seller's favour.
- Prices, dates and statistics match their source; market stats name the source and period.
- Photos and renderings are accurate; virtual staging or renderings are labelled as such. No altered photos that hide defects or change views.
- Sold prices and "sold" claims only as permitted, with the seller's consent where required.

## Human Rights Code
- Describe the property, not the ideal buyer or tenant. No wording about age, family status, religion, ancestry, disability, sex, sexual orientation, etc. ("perfect for young professionals", "adult-oriented", "Christian neighbourhood").
- Strata age or rental restrictions are stated as facts of the bylaws ("19+ strata per bylaws"), not as preferences.

## Trademarks
- MLS® and REALTOR® take the ® and are used only as CREA allows (MLS® System, REALTOR® member). Never "MLS listing" as a generic noun without the mark in formal material.

## Email and privacy
- CASL: commercial emails need consent, sender identification and a working unsubscribe.
- No client personal information in public material without written consent; testimonials and photos of people need permission.

## Output for a review
Verdict (ready / ready with fixes / not ready), then numbered issues: location, problem, fix, rule.
