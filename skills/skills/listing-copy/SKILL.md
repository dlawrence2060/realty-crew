# Listing copy

## Before writing
1. Gather facts from what's provided: data sheet, floor plan, strata docs, BC Assessment, photos, the realtor's notes. List them; anything else is unknown.
2. Find the voice: grep library/website/property/ for two or three past listings of the same type and area, and read library/brand/ (banned words, tone).
3. Check library/listings/README.md for the board's current character limits and any house style.

## Structure for public remarks
- Open with the single most distinctive thing (view, location, architecture, renovation), not "Welcome to".
- Then the living experience: layout, light, kitchen, principal bedroom, outdoor space.
- Then the practical: size (with source), bedrooms/bathrooms, parking, storage, strata highlights (fees, rentals/pets bylaws as facts), schools/transit/amenities by name.
- Close with a clear next step (showings by appointment, open house date).
- Respect the character limit; give the count.

## Style
- Specific beats superlative: "11-foot ceilings" not "soaring ceilings"; "west-facing 400 sq. ft. terrace" not "amazing outdoor space".
- Canadian spelling. Numbers: sq. ft., numerals for measurements, $ with commas.
- No clichés unless the brand guide allows them: stunning, nestled, boasts, must-see, won't last, pride of ownership, turnkey dream.
- No steering language (see bcfsa-advertising).

## Facts
- Every claim traceable. Unknowns as [CONFIRM: ...], listed at the end.
- Measurements carry their source and "approx." when appropriate.
- Never mention defects, the seller's circumstances or price motivation in public remarks; those go to the realtor.
