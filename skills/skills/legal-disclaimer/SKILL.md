# Legal information, not legal advice

Nothing the crew produces is legal advice. The crew are AI agents, not lawyers, notaries or licensed advisers.

## The disclaimer
Put this, unchanged, at the top of every file that explains law, rules, contracts, tenancy, taxes or drafts legal wording, and repeat its first sentence in your report:

> **General information only, not legal advice.** This was prepared by an AI assistant to help a REALTOR® understand the topic. It may be incomplete or out of date, and it doesn't consider every fact of your situation. Before acting, confirm against the official source cited and get advice from a BC lawyer or notary (or the Residential Tenancy Branch for tenancy matters).

Drafted wording (clauses, addenda, letters, notices) is additionally headed **DRAFT FOR REVIEW BY A LAWYER OR NOTARY. NOT FOR USE AS IS.**

## Sourcing
- Every rule, deadline, rate or limit cites its source: statute and section (bclaws.gov.bc.ca), regulation, RTB policy guideline number, BCFSA rule or guidance page, or gov.bc.ca page, with the date you checked it.
- Prefer the library (library/legal/, library/tenancy/) when it holds the official document; check its date. Prefer official sources over blogs and law-firm articles.
- Figures that change yearly (allowable rent increase, tax thresholds, deposit rules) must be looked up, not remembered. If you can't verify one, write [CONFIRM: ...] and say so.

## Escalate to a lawyer, say it first
Put a clear "Talk to a lawyer" line at the top when: a dispute exists or is likely, a deadline has been or is about to be missed, money is at risk (deposits, damages, collapsed deals), someone has been served with legal papers, liability or professional discipline could be involved, or the answer turns on facts nobody has confirmed.

## Never
- Tell someone what they "should" do legally, predict a ruling, or say something is "legal" or "safe" without qualification.
- Invent a notice form; point to the official RTB or BCREA form.
- Give tax or immigration advice beyond general, cited information.
